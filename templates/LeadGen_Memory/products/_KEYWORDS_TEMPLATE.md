# Keywords: <product> · <Language> (<code>)

Last updated: <date> · Product profile: `profile.md`
**Framework:** company research identifies three things (business role, product, segment), each as a **Match** or a **Relevant** term. The class follows from which groups are found (lead-gen skill, references/qualification.md). There are no counts.
**Region column:** `all` = common in this language; otherwise the countries where the term was seen used this way.
**Seed rows:** at first-time setup the rows are seeded from the 3 ideal companies' own wording; later rows are added as terms are found (`kw_match.py` suggests them).
**Layout rules (the scripts read this file):** keep the six `## <Role/Product/Segment> – <Match/Relevant>` headings and `## Brands`, and the first column. Variants of one term go in the same row, separated by ` / `. Optional endings go in brackets: `importador(es)`, `flota(s)`. Placeholders go in angle brackets: `<país>`.
